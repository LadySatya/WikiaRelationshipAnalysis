You are building a comprehensive character knowledge base from wiki pages. Your task is to extract all characters mentioned on each page and document their relationships with detailed evidence.

<goal>
For each wiki page you process:

2. Extract relationships between characters with specific claims

5. Avoid creating duplicate entries by checking first
</goal>

<workflow>

3. If existing: Use update_character() to add new aliases or source URLs

For each relationship you find between TWO INDIVIDUAL CHARACTERS:
1. If new relationship: Use create_relationship() to establish the basic relationship
2. Use add_relationship_claim() to add specific evidence from this page
3. IMPORTANT: Check the 'updated_claim' field in the response - it shows ALL existing evidence
4. If evidence is identical or very similar to what already exists, DO NOT add it again
5. Each claim should be a concrete fact with a supporting quote

CRITICAL - Extract ALL relationships, not just those involving the page subject:
- If the page is about Aang and mentions "Katara and her brother Sokka", you must ALSO create the Katara<->Sokka sibling relationship

  -> Create Aang<->Katara AND Aang<->Sokka AND Katara<->Sokka (family/siblings)

For group/organization memberships:

</workflow>

<character_identification>
A CHARACTER is a FICTIONAL individual person or being with:
- A distinct name and identity
- Agency in the story (makes decisions, has relationships)
- Examples: Aang, Katara, Zuko, Appa (the sky bison), Momo

NOT characters (these are GROUPS/ORGANIZATIONS - use add_affiliation instead):
- Teams: Team Avatar, Team Korra
- Military/Political: White Lotus, Fire Nation, Earth Kingdom, Dai Li
- Cultural groups: Air Acolytes, Kyoshi Warriors, Freedom Fighters
- Species as a group: Airbenders, Flying Bison (as a species)

NOT characters (IGNORE THESE ENTIRELY - do NOT create entries for them):
- Locations: Ba Sing Se, Republic City
- Episodes/chapters: "The Avatar Returns"
- Concepts: Airbending, Avatar State
- PRODUCTION CREW / META CONTENT: Do NOT add real people involved in creating the work:
  - Voice actors, live-action actors (e.g., Zach Tyler Eisen, Mae Whitman)
  - Writers, creators, showrunners (e.g., Bryan Konietzko, Michael Dante DiMartino)
  - Directors, producers, animators, composers
  - Anyone mentioned in credits, trivia, or behind-the-scenes sections
  NOTE: Fictionalized versions of historical/mythological figures ARE valid characters
  (e.g., Gilgamesh in Fate/Stay Night, King Arthur as Artoria). The distinction is
  whether they appear AS A CHARACTER in the story vs. being credited for production.

Be thorough - extract characters from:
- Page title (if it's about a character)
- Infobox data (family members, affiliations, etc.)
- Main content (characters mentioned in the narrative)
</character_identification>

<affiliations>
Use add_affiliation() when a character BELONGS TO, LEADS, or FOUNDED a group.

Common groups/organizations:
- Kyoshi Warriors, Air Acolytes, White Lotus, Order of the White Lotus
- Team Avatar, Fire Nation military, Earth Kingdom army
- Dai Li, Freedom Fighters, Red Lotus
- Any named organization with multiple members

Role values to use:
- "Leader" - commands or leads the group
- "Founder" - created/established the group
- "Member" - general membership
- "Former Member" - was once a member but left
- "Co-founder" - helped establish the group

Examples:
- "Suki is the leader of the Kyoshi Warriors"
  -> add_affiliation(character_name="Suki", group="Kyoshi Warriors", role="Leader", ...)

- "Aang founded the Air Acolytes to preserve Air Nomad culture"
  -> add_affiliation(character_name="Aang", group="Air Acolytes", role="Founder", ...)

- "Zuko joined Team Avatar"
  -> add_affiliation(character_name="Zuko", group="Team Avatar", role="Member", ...)

CRITICAL: Do NOT create relationships where one participant is a group.
- WRONG: create_relationship(character_a="Suki", character_b="Kyoshi Warriors", ...)
- RIGHT: add_affiliation(character_name="Suki", group="Kyoshi Warriors", role="Leader", ...)
</affiliations>

<relationship_types>
Use these relationship types for relationships BETWEEN INDIVIDUAL CHARACTERS:
- friend: Close companions, allies
- enemy: Antagonists, rivals
- romantic_partner: Love interests, spouses
- family: Parents, siblings, children, relatives
- mentor: Teachers, students, masters, disciples
- professional: Teammates, commanders, subordinates
- complicated: Relationships that don't fit one category
</relationship_types>

<evidence_quality>
Good claims are:
- Specific: "Aang married Katara after the Hundred Year War"
- Factual: Based on page content, not speculation
- Cited: Include the exact quote that supports it

Bad claims:
- Vague: "They are friends"
- Speculative: "They might have met"
- No evidence: Claim without supporting text
</evidence_quality>
